# Review 05: Audit log CSV export

**Verdict: FIX FIRST, 6/10.** The formula-injection defence works and access control is correct. The problems are privacy leftovers (browser cache, free-text PII in the log) and an export that is wrong for Excel/Bangla and silent about truncation.

## What I ran
- `repro/05-audit-csv.js` starts the real server on port 8105 and drives the real `auditCsv()` in headless Chromium. It parses the downloaded CSV with an RFC4180 parser and probes auth, localStorage and a 5000-entry log.
- `repro/05-audit-pii.js` covers account deletion (A31), the localStorage caches, and which actions are not audited.
- Run both with `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node <file>`.

## What holds up (tested)
- Guest, non-admin and bogus-token calls to `auditall` all get `forbidden` (400); admin gets 200. The cache is `no-store`.
- Formula injection: of 12 hostile admin names I tried, 8 started with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@` or a quote and all were neutralised. None survived at the start of a cell. The `\t` and `\r` variants are trimmed by `rename`. The details column always starts with `{`, so its commas and quotes round-trip through the parser and cannot start a formula. Newlines in JSON args are quoted correctly.
- Fullwidth `＝` and a leading space or newline before `=` stayed as plain text, so no injection (the leading-space and newline case is untested in real Excel).
- Admin names are cut to 24 characters. A deleted admin's name is scrubbed to `—` in the log.

## Does this item matter?
Yes, moderately. The two-admin correction rule and manual balance adjustments are the only real-trust controls in a play-money game. The log is the evidence trail for disputes, so it deserves to be accurate (F4, F5). The export is a convenience for 2 to 5 admins. The real risks are not formula injection, which is handled. They are the client-side copies of admin and player data after logout or deletion (F1, F2) and a log that cannot prove who did what. With about 10,000 players and a handful of admins, the 1.5 MB size is tolerable for now.
